# 🧠 Promptopia

**Promptopia** is a modern, free platform for discovering and sharing AI prompts. Whether you're an AI enthusiast, creator, or just looking for inspiration, Promptopia makes it easy to explore, create, and manage high-quality prompts.

![Promptopia Logo](#) <!-- coming soon -->

## 🚀 Features

- 🔍 **Search Prompts** – Find prompts by keywords or user names.
- ✏️ **Create Prompts** – Share your own creative AI prompts with the community.
- 🛠️ **Edit & Delete** – Manage your prompts with full control.
- 👥 **User Profiles** – View all prompts shared by a specific user.
